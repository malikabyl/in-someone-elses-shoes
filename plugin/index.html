In Someone Else’s Shoes

A free historical financial life simulation by ND SaaS Solutions LLP (ТОО «ND SaaS Solutions»), Kazakhstan.

Choose a career, manage simulated assets and debt, advance through historical quarters, and compare nominal and inflation-adjusted outcomes. All trades are fictional.

  • Play USA, 1971–1991
  • Play Japan, 1980–2005

The ChatGPT plugin connects to the game's MCP service for in-chat play. Its public listing is being prepared; this page does not announce approval or availability in the plugin directory.

No payment is required. Donations are not connected.
