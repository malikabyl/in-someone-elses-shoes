Support — Preparation

In Someone Else’s Shoes is a free historical financial life game from ND SaaS Solutions LLP, Kazakhstan.

Troubleshooting

  • Choose USA or Japan and a profession returned by the game.
  • If a trade is refused, read the returned reason: funds, ownership, lending limits and historical restrictions can affect actions.
  • Do not repeat an uncertain action as a new action; retries should reuse the original request identifier.
  • Browser progress belongs to that browser's site storage. Clearing it can remove the local save.

Reporting a problem

Prepare the game country, quarter, action and exact error text. Remove session identifiers, private chat content and account details before sharing screenshots.

Contact support or ask about your data

Email info@ndbase.kz for game support and requests about access, correction or deletion of game data. Identify the game edition and describe your request. Do not include passwords or session identifiers in public reports. If additional information is needed to locate a session, arrange it privately with support. No unverified response time or immediate deletion promise is made.

Ending a game does not immediately delete its server data. See the draft privacy notice for the 30-day session timer and unresolved infrastructure retention.
